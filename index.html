Frequently Asked Questions

General

Solvian is your comprehensive productivity and service platform designed to empower your daily workflows with fast, secure, and intuitive tools.

Download or open the app, complete your initial account registration, verify your credentials, and start exploring tools from the home dashboard.

Tap the 🌐 globe icon in the top bar anytime to choose English, বাংলা, or العربية. The interface updates instantly.

Go to your Profile and select Settings to adjust notifications, display themes, security preferences, and cloud sync options.

Account & Privacy

Open Settings > Account Management to update your email, linked phone number, password, or configure Two-Factor Authentication.

Solvian uses TLS 1.3 encryption for data in transit and AES-256 for data at rest. We never share your private records with third parties.

Troubleshooting

Try closing and reopening the app, clear the cache from Phone Settings > Apps > Solvian, or switch to the "Report a Problem" tab to send a diagnostic report.
